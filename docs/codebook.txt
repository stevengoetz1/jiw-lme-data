llm_recoding_counts.csv  (added 2026-11-05)
============================================================
One row per analysis deal (275 of 277; two US Renal Care rows had no agency text).
Three LLM "coders" re-coded cause of distress from coding_inputs.csv (agency quotes, EBITDA
trend, secondary factors; blind to the existing code, deal form and outcome) using the Appendix A
codebook: gpt-4o-mini (30 runs/deal), gpt-4.1-mini (30), gpt-4o (10), temperature 1.
  A_<model>, B_<model>, C_<model>  = number of runs landing on each group
  p_A, p_B, p_C   = pooled share of all 70 runs
  llm_modal       = group with the highest pooled share
  llm_maxp        = that share (>= 0.8 for 74% of deals)
  cause_code / cause_group = the dataset's (coder #1) code, for comparison
  *_gpt4omini_masked = same model, 30 runs, with the company name scrubbed from the prompt and quotes and the
                       date rounded to the year (name-leakage check; 91% of modal labels unchanged)
Full per-run output with one-sentence rationales: llm_codes.csv (download from the Colab Files pane).

data/analysis/deals_scored.csv  (built by code/01_build_score.py; do not edit by hand)
============================================================
One row per analysis deal (277). All columns of lme_deals_clean.csv, plus:
  score            structural score, 0-1: share of the 70 re-codings that say structural, mixed = one half (blank for 2 deals)
  score_noC        the same ignoring mixed codings
  score_<model>    the score from one model's runs alone
  score_masked     gpt-4o-mini score with company names removed from the text
  struct_major     1 if score > 0.5 (binary, majority wins)
  score_bin        Low (0-0.33) / Middle (0.33-0.67) / High (0.67-1)
  coder1_score     original three labels on the same scale (A = 0, C = 0.5, B = 1)
  form             LME / Pro rata
  repurchase       1 = open-market repurchase (no negotiated terms)
  sp_listed        1 = the deal appears in S&P's default tables
  needs_review     1 = re-check this row before the final paper; review_reason says why

data/analysis/terms_scored.csv  (built by code/01_build_score.py)
One row per negotiated exchange (200). All columns of lme_terms_clean.csv, plus score, score_bin, struct_major,
  strict_klk       strictness index (mean of standardised terms, higher = tougher on the borrower; pro rata deals only)
  fix              1 = haircut or equitisation
  runway_only      1 = extension or PIK with no debt cut

data/coded/llm_recoding_counts_binary.csv  (added 2026-11-12)
============================================================
Two-label re-coding: the same inputs, models and run counts as llm_recoding_counts.csv (gpt-4o-mini 30, gpt-4.1-mini 30,
gpt-4o 10, temperature 1), but the coder must answer shock (A) or structural (B); there is no mixed option.
  A2_<model>, B2_<model> = number of runs landing on each group (they sum to the model's run count)
  two-label score = (sum of B2 columns) / 70
Produced by "Part 5" of the JIW Database Colab notebook; per-run output with rationales is llm_codes_binary.csv in that Colab session.
